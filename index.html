Captain Emergency & Incident Reporting

Guidelines for emergencies, accidents and safety incidents

1. Purpose

This policy explains what Heartifo Captains should do when an emergency, accident, safety incident or other serious event occurs while using the Heartifo platform.

2. Immediate Emergency

If there is an immediate danger to life or safety, Captains should contact the appropriate local emergency service first.

Heartifo Support should be contacted after immediate safety needs have been addressed, where reasonably possible.

3. Accidents

If a road accident occurs during a Heartifo ride, the Captain should prioritize the safety of everyone involved.

  • Move to a safe location when reasonably possible.
  • Seek medical assistance when necessary.
  • Contact appropriate emergency services where required.
  • Report the incident to Heartifo as soon as reasonably possible.

4. Safety Incidents

Captains should report serious incidents including threats, violence, harassment, dangerous situations, major vehicle problems or other events that may affect safety.

5. Customer Medical Emergency

If a customer experiences a medical emergency during a ride, the Captain should take reasonable steps to obtain appropriate emergency or medical assistance.

6. Vehicle Breakdown

If the vehicle develops a serious mechanical problem during a ride, the Captain should stop in a safe location when reasonably possible.

The Captain should inform the customer and contact Heartifo Support when appropriate.

7. Dangerous Road Conditions

Captains should use reasonable judgment when weather, traffic, road conditions or other circumstances create a significant safety concern.

A Captain should not knowingly continue a ride when doing so would create an unreasonable safety risk.

8. Incident Information

When reporting an incident, Heartifo may request information such as:

  • Captain account information
  • Ride or booking details
  • Date and approximate time
  • Location of the incident
  • Description of what happened
  • Vehicle information
  • Photographs or supporting documents
  • Emergency or police information, where applicable

9. Accurate Reporting

Captains should provide accurate and honest information when reporting an emergency, accident or safety incident.

False, altered or intentionally misleading information may result in account review or other action under applicable Heartifo policies.

10. Cooperation With Reviews

Heartifo may review relevant information following a reported incident.

Captains may be asked to provide reasonable information or documentation to assist with the review.

11. Police or Government Authorities

Where required by applicable law, Captains should cooperate with appropriate authorities regarding accidents or other incidents.

12. Insurance-Related Incidents

Where an applicable insurance arrangement exists, Captains should follow the relevant insurer's reporting and claim requirements.

Insurance coverage remains subject to the applicable policy terms, conditions and eligibility requirements.

13. Confidentiality and Privacy

Information about customers, Captains and incidents should be handled responsibly and only used for legitimate purposes connected with the incident or support process.

14. Non-Emergency Support

For non-urgent issues such as technical problems, ride questions, document issues or earnings concerns, Captains should use the normal Heartifo Support process.

15. Safety-Related Account Review

Serious safety incidents may result in temporary restrictions, suspension or other account review while Heartifo assesses the circumstances.

16. Policy Changes

Heartifo may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in services, applicable requirements or operational practices.

17. Contact Heartifo

For accident reporting, emergency-related support or safety incident assistance, please contact Heartifo.
